Guest mode

Guest mode keeps nothing safe.

You can train without an account. This is what it costs, in plain terms.

  • Your data lives only in this browser Nothing leaves the phone. There is no copy on our side.
  • Clearing site data destroys it Clearing history, a private window closing, or the browser reclaiming storage takes every workout with it.
  • Nothing syncs to another device Your phone and your laptop keep separate logs. They cannot be merged later.
  • Lose the phone, lose the history There is no recovery, no reset link, and no support route back to it.
You can create an account laterGuest workouts move across when you do, as long as this browser still has them.

An account takes an email and a password. We recommend it.
