# The ten journeys

Each tester takes one. They are people with something to get done, not
checklists of screens.

---

**1. The lifter mid-session.** It is Wednesday, you are at the gym, and you
are training Push. Start the session from wherever the app offers it. Log
every set of the first two exercises for real — weights, reps, the done
tick. Use the rest timer. Swap an exercise. Add one. Take a set off. Then
finish and read the review. **Arithmetic:** does the review's volume equal
the sum of every weight x reps you ticked? Does the set count match? Does
the duration match the timer? If a PR fires, is it actually your best?

**2. The person logging a day of food.** You ate four times today. Log a
meal by barcode, one by search, one from the things you log often, and one
serving of a recipe. Add water twice. Tick a supplement. **Arithmetic:**
after every single log, does the day's remaining-calorie figure move by
exactly what you logged? Do the macro bars move by the right grams? Does
protein-remaining agree with protein-eaten against the target? Now delete
one and check it all moves back.

**3. The returning user.** You have not opened the app in a week. Read
Recap at all three scales. Tap into a day from the month calendar. Open a
session from there. **Arithmetic:** does the week's total equal the sum of
its sessions? Does the month's equal the sum of its weeks? Does a session
opened from the calendar show the same figures as the same session opened
from Train's history? Step back through previous weeks and months — do the
figures stay sane, and does an empty week say so rather than showing zero
as if it were a result?

**4. The planner.** You want a new training week. Build a split from
scratch: name it, add days, add exercises to each, reorder them, delete one,
undo that. Then edit an existing split. Then start today's session from it.
**Arithmetic:** does the weekly volume readout match the exercises you
placed? Do the warnings fire on the right thresholds? Does the split you
built actually drive the session that starts?

**5. The person tracking a body.** Log a body weight. Read the trend. Open
Goals, add one, check its progress figure. Open Photos, look at the grid,
open one, try Analyse, decline the consent, then delete a photo.
**Arithmetic:** does the weight delta match first-to-last in the log? Does
the goal's percentage match its own numbers? Is the trend line drawn to the
same scale as its labels?

**6. The interrupted lifter.** Start a workout. Now leave it — go to every
single tab and every pushed screen you can reach. On each one, is the
session shelf there, does it name the right session, does its clock keep
moving, and does it take you back? Come back and check nothing was lost.
Then finish. Then check the shelf is gone everywhere. Then start another,
discard it, and check the same.

**7. The new user.** You have never opened this before. Onboarding from the
first screen to the last, then the tutorial, then your first session. Answer
honestly and see whether the app is actually set up for you at the end.
Then do it again and skip everything you can — does skipping leave a usable
app or a broken one? **Check:** does anything the tutorial claims not exist?

**8. The shopper.** You are going to the supermarket. Open Shopping. Build a
list, check things off, undo, clear the done ones. Look at Pantry, Stores,
Receipts. Set a budget and check the running total. **Arithmetic:** does the
budget remaining equal the cap minus what is spent? Does the list count
match the rows? Does clearing the done items remove exactly those?

**9. The Stack user.** Turn on Stack tracking in Settings. Open it. Read the
active cycle, its overview and the clearance dates. Add a compound from the
preset list and one by hand. Mark today's dose taken and un-take it. End the
cycle, then reopen it, then start a new one. **Arithmetic:** does each
clearance date equal the last scheduled dose plus 5.5 half-lives? Does the
weekly total equal the dose times the schedule? Do the "due today" rows
match each compound's own frequency from its own start date?

**10. The auditor.** You are not walking a journey. You are checking that
the app tells one story. Take every figure that appears on more than one
screen — the last session, the week's volume, body weight, the day's
calories, PRs, the exercise count, the streak — and check every screen that
shows it agrees. Also check every unit in the build converts when the unit
setting changes, and that no screen shows a figure the fixture cannot
support. Where two screens disagree, work out which one is wrong.
