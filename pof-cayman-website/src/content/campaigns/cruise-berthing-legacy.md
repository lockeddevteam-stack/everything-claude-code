Protect Our Future started in 2018 as a campaign against overdevelopment. The proposed cruise berthing facility in George Town Harbour became the fight that defined the organisation.

On 30 April 2025, the Cayman Islands held a referendum. 11,993 people, 63.66% of those who voted, said no to cruise berthing. 5,417 people, 28.75%, said yes.

George Town Harbour is a Mission Blue Hope Spot, recognised for its reef and marine life. POF's position was that the piers would have destroyed reef the islands cannot replace.

## What POF did

- Spoke at public meetings and to the press from 2018 onward.
- Painted the banners that became the campaign's most photographed image.
- Took the case to the UN climate conferences at COP25, COP26, COP27 and COP28.
- Kept the issue in front of students at four high schools.

## What happens next

A referendum result is a starting point. POF keeps watching development proposals for George Town Harbour and reports on them here and on Instagram.
