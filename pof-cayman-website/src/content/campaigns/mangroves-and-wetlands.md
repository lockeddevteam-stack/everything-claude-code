Grand Cayman's mangroves protect the island from storm surge, filter the water that reaches the reef, and give young fish somewhere to grow before they move out to open water. Clearing them for development removes all three at once.

"Mangroves Secure Our Future" is one of the slogans painted on POF's banners. It is also the argument in plain terms.

## What POF has done

- Submitted a formal letter on amendments to the National Conservation Act.
- Partnered with the Mangrove Action Project and the National Trust for the Cayman Islands.
