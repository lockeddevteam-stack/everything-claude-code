"Our Future is Not Single-Use" is painted on one of POF's banners. It is the campaign in five words.

On World Cleanup Day, POF and its partners put 300 volunteers on 13 locations around the islands and removed over two tons of plastic. Cleanups matter, and they also show what a ban would stop at the source.

## What POF has done

- Called publicly for a national single-use plastic ban.
- Organised and joined cleanups with Plastic Free Cayman and other partners.
- Worked with schools to cut single-use plastic on campus.

## What you can do

Bring a reusable bottle. Then come to the next cleanup on the Events page and see what a morning's work looks like.
