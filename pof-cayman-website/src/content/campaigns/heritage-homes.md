"Protecting Our Future Is Protecting Our Past." Cayman's traditional wooden homes were built for this climate by the people who lived here before the tourism economy. They are being lost to the same development pressure that threatens the coastline.

## Why an environmental group cares

Heritage and environment are the same argument here. Both ask what the islands keep for the next generation, and both are decided by the same planning process.

## What you can do

If your family has a heritage home or a story about one, POF wants to hear it. Write to us through the Contact page.
